Unicode Character Database snapshot used by cellstr
===================================================

Unicode version: 18.0.0
Fetched: 2026-10-12 (UTC), unmodified, with curl from these URLs:

  EastAsianWidth.txt (UAX #11, East_Asian_Width)
    https://www.unicode.org/Public/UCD/latest/ucd/EastAsianWidth.txt
  DerivedGeneralCategory.txt (Mn, Me, Cf and friends)
    https://www.unicode.org/Public/UCD/latest/ucd/extracted/DerivedGeneralCategory.txt
  emoji-data.txt (Emoji, Emoji_Presentation, Emoji_Modifier, Extended_Pictographic)
    https://www.unicode.org/Public/UCD/latest/ucd/emoji/emoji-data.txt
  GraphemeBreakProperty.txt (Grapheme_Cluster_Break)
    https://www.unicode.org/Public/UCD/latest/ucd/auxiliary/GraphemeBreakProperty.txt
  DerivedCoreProperties.txt (Indic_Conjunct_Break, InCB)
    https://www.unicode.org/Public/UCD/latest/ucd/DerivedCoreProperties.txt
  GraphemeBreakTest.txt (official segmentation test cases)
    https://www.unicode.org/Public/UCD/latest/ucd/auxiliary/GraphemeBreakTest.txt

Specifications: UAX #11 East Asian Width https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr11/ ,
UAX #29 Unicode Text Segmentation https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr29/ and
UTS #51 Unicode Emoji https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr51/ .

The width rules follow Markus Kuhn's reference wcwidth, fetched the same day from
https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~mgk25/ucs/wcwidth.c (see GUIDE.md for the rules and the
documented departures).

scripts/gen_ucd_tables.py turns the property files into src/cellstr/_ucd_data.py and
src/cellstr/_ucd_part*.py. tests/test_ucd_tables.py checks that the committed tables
match a fresh run of the generator, and tests/test_grapheme_conformance.py runs every
case in GraphemeBreakTest.txt.
